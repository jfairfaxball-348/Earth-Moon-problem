# Independent S035 resumption audit

This is an integrity, SAT-signal and lifecycle-source review. No original process
was resumed, suspended, signalled or terminated by this audit. No live campaign
file or certificate status was changed.

`SAT_RESCAN.json` hashes 4,799 scientific log/JSON files from the campaign,
three benchmarks, imported S034 and both retained phase diagnostics. No
SATISFIABLE marker, exit-code10 record or candidate was found. The exact
unresolved IDs remain 0,1,3,7,15,31,63,127,255; 247 encoded regions are recorded
proof-closed and no pending proof recipe exists. The 4,705 hit-bearing files in
isolated control areas are counted separately. This is not fresh DRAT replay.

The historical watcher glob `attempts/cell-*/*/*` correctly matches the existing
cell-directory/token/file layout. Its relevant coverage limits are roots outside
the selected campaign, candidate/result name variations and failure handling.
The historical resident visit handles an ordinary stop reason before checking
returncode10, and an independent-validator exception can precede a candidate
record. The new external watcher must therefore preserve SAT evidence before
validation, and SAT must take priority over simultaneous STOP/UNKNOWN.

`tools/s035_sat_validation.py` binds complete consistent assignment bytes to the
strict native header, ordered edge-pair mapping, variables, declared and actual
clause counts, exact immutable INPUT recipe, source/base hashes, pinned scientific
mapping, native and historical source hashes. It checks region assumptions and
graph-unit pins explicitly, then uses the historical independent checker for CNF,
both planar layers, edge-disjoint union, order28, alpha<=3, S11 degree and edge
domain, and a proper colouring. Full assignment/raw assignment lines and hash
diagnostics survive a rejection. A passing candidate still requires prominent
domain review; no mathematical headline is promoted.

Thirteen small validator controls pass, including a nonplanar K5 rejection and
assignment preservation when the exact input is missing. They
launch zero native workers. `RECIPE_BINDING.json` independently confirms that
the four retained native inputs exactly match their immutable original recipes
and recorded SHA256 values. Later appended resident deltas must never replace
the original INPUT recipe used for that binding.

The retained native contexts are7/PID12384 and127/PID3500, previously PARKED,
and0/PID14812 and255/PID17984, previously RUNNING. Their commands use
`--timeout inf`; the stale controller's phase deadlines must not be resumed.
The old guardian, controller and console identities must stay suspended with
historical STOP latched. A separate controller can use a new identity registry,
fresh monotonic active turns and a separate pause token without releasing stale
claims. Actual per-thread creation identities and suspend counts are required;
the documentary PARKED/RUNNING labels alone do not determine current counts.

The proposed initial diagnostic is a bounded, same-process retention pilot with
at most two executing identities and all four resident admissions retained.
Every identity must receive a turn and resume once before interpreting telemetry.
Register pre/post native conflicts, decisions, propagations and process CPU time,
paused CPU, private RAM and complete geometric-clause prefix hashes by region.
Compare deltas per active CPU second within each region; do not compare raw
counters across regions or infer completion/ETA. Congruence remains disabled and
random walk remains default. A new branch/inprocessing comparison requires a
separate preregistration and available capacity; cold1,3,15,31,63 stay queued.

No solver/proof/checker is launched by the preservation controller. On a natural
native result it must suspend every surviving resident, retain the exact exit
status, log/input/recipe and new-clause prefixes, and defer UNSAT promotion to a
fresh standalone DRAT proof and independent checker. An old loaded executable
cannot acquire a cooperative exit hook retroactively. Non-forcing suspension
preserves its RAM; only a natural native exit is available without replacing or
discarding that private state.
