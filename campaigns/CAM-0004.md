# CAM-0004 — exhaustive clique blow-ups of all bases on at most 8 vertices

Registered in S003 before execution.

## Question

For a graph B on k vertices and weights w in Z_{>=0}^k, B[w] replaces vertex
i by a clique of w_i vertices and joins adjacent bags completely. Is there
any biplanar B[w] with chi>=10 when k<=8? (S002 settled all odd cycles;
Szeider settles alpha(B)<=2 source-stated; this domain contains both and far
more, e.g. all alpha>=3 non-cycle bases on <=8 vertices.)

## Reductions (proved in research/S003_BLOWUP_CENSUS.md before use)

Zero weights pass to the induced base; disconnected bases to components;
true twins (adjacent, equal closed neighbourhoods) merge into one bag. No
perfection/SPGT shortcut is used: perfect bases are processed like the rest.
Hence it suffices to treat connected, true-twin-free bases on 1..8 vertices (generated by nauty geng -c, nauty 2.8.8 from the pynauty
2.8.8.1 sdist; connected counts 21/112/853/11117 for k=5..8 checked against OEIS A001349) with
positive weights. Any w_i>=9 contains K9, so weights are 1..8.

Core reduction (added before the k>=7 runs, after a k<=6 timing trial of
the first version): if a bag has closed weighted degree w_i+sum_{j~i}w_j<=9
it can be coloured last, so a chi>=10 blow-up keeps chi>=10 after repeatedly
deleting such bags; the residue is a blow-up of an induced base (components,
twin merges) in which every bag has closed weighted degree >=10. Vectors
failing this are recorded as LOWDEG; they are never claimed colourable on
their own, but are covered by the core argument.

## Necessary biplanarity filters (each a theorem, never a heuristic)

F1 weighted clique number <=8 (K9 is not biplanar).
F2 every union of full bags X with |X|>=3 has e(X)<=6|X|-12.
F3 for every set S of bags and every triangle-free edge set F of B[S], the
   cross edges along F form a triangle-free graph, so their number is
   <=4|S|-8 when |S|>=3 (triangle-free planar layer <=2n-4, two layers).
F4 chi(B[w])>=10, decided exactly: an explicit 9-colouring (greedy, else
   SAT model) refutes it; survivors need a 9-colour UNSAT (DRAT-checked) or
   the elementary bound N>9*alpha(B).

Survivors of F1-F4 are candidates, not witnesses: they would then go to the
CAM-0003 biplanarity oracle and further obstructions.

## Independent validation

A second, independently written checker (tools/blowup_census_check.py)
re-derives the result by a different route: brute enumeration over all
weight vectors with plain product iteration (no DFS pruning), F1/F2 by
direct clique/subset enumeration, and an independent weighted colouring
(exact DP over independent-set multiplicities) for every F1-F3 survivor.
Counts of bases and of filter survivors must agree.

## Resources and stopping

Python 3.13.16, python-sat 1.9.dev15, NetworkX 3.5; 4 cores; per-run
wall cap 4 hours. k=9 (261080 connected bases) only if k<=8 completes and
time remains; otherwise unexamined, not negative. A timeout leaves the
unreached part UNKNOWN. Outcome scope: exactly the clique blow-ups of
graphs on at most 8 (or 9 if run) vertices; no claim about partial joins,
other substitutions or larger bases.
