# CAM-0027 — S025 Arm A: chromatic-certificate annealing over sphere-triangulation pairs

**Purpose.** A positive χ ≥ 10 search with an exact chromatic objective, replacing the S024 overlap objective (F068).

**Engine.** `tools/s025_indep_anneal.py`.
- State: two labelled sphere triangulations on n vertices. Moves: edge flips.
- Objective: the number of independent sets of weight ≥ W+1 in the union (the first k vertices weigh 2, the rest 1).
- Zero means χ ≥ ⌈(n+k)/W⌉, re-checked elementarily by `tools/s025_weighted_check.py`. Nothing reached the χ ≥ 10 range, so no χ ≥ 10 check was triggered.

**Calibration.**
- Each corpus row has χ_f = 28/3 (269 rows) or 37/4 (2 rows): an exploratory LP with system scipy, not a certificate. So corpus χ ≥ 10 is a W=3 total-28 weighted certificate, the class targeted here.
- Szeider: α≤2 ⇒ 9-colourable. Our α≤2 annealing reaches 0 at n=16 and bottoms at 1 and 2 at n=17, 18.

**Runs.** Exploratory, NOT preregistered before the session. Parameters and outcomes are in validation/S025/ARM_A_FRONTIER.json: 900–1800 s each, seeds 1–3, light workers.

| W | Configuration | Best minimum |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | n = 22, 24 | 0 (explicit α=3 graphs; the n=24 graph has χ=8 by an exhibited colouring) |
| 3 | n = 25, 26, 27, 28 | 1, 4, 13, 35–58 |
| 3, total weight 28 | (n,k) = (22,6), (23,5), (24,4), (25,3), (26,2), (27,1) | 8, 10, 13, 21, 17, 19 |
| 4 | n = 30, 32 | 0 |
| 4 | n = 34, 36, 37 (one short run each) | 773, 1873, 2369 |

The three 1800 s α=4 reruns (n = 33–35) were lost when the session restarted (NOT_COMPLETED).

The naive colouring-CEGAR `chrom` mode of `tools/s025_tricover.py` did not even settle n=10/11, k=9 in 500 s (F069).

**Decision.** There is no χ ≥ 10 witness. These are heuristic minima, not non-existence results. Fractional certificates at W=3/4 are far from reachable by this search at the tested sizes.
