# CAM-0003 — proof-producing Schnyder-order biplanarity oracle on fixed graphs

Registered in S003 before execution.

## Encoding (generator: tools/biplanar_cnf.py)

Input: a simple graph G on V={0..n-1}. Variables: x_e (edge e in layer 1,
else layer 2); for each layer L in {1,2} and order i in {1,2,3}, a strict
linear order on V encoded by p[L,i,a,b] for a<b (a below b), with full
transitivity clauses. For each edge e={u,v}, layer L, vertex w not in e:
the clause "e in L implies OR_i (u <_{L,i} w and v <_{L,i} w)", with auxiliary
conjunction variables defined by implications only (aux -> both literals).
Soundness: by Schnyder's theorem (Order 5 (1989) 323-343) a graph is planar
iff its dimension (as defined by Schnyder through suitable vertex orders)
is at most 3, and suitability is inherited by spanning subgraphs; hence the
CNF is satisfiable iff G is biplanar. Only symmetry breaking: one fixed edge
(the lexicographically first) is forced into layer 1, sound by layer swap.

## Domain (fixed explicit graphs only)

1. Controls: K8 (expect SAT), K9 (expect UNSAT, known theorem), Sulanke
   K6+C5 from validation/sulanke.json (expect SAT), K_{5,13}? not used.
   Encoding cross-validation of the single-layer planarity sub-encoding on
   300 seeded random graphs n=6..10 against NetworkX LR (seed 3).
2. apex(C5[K3]): C5[K3] with a universal vertex (16 vertices, 75 edges).
3. Apex lane: every graph in Patil corpus data/graphs/pool/*.edges (commit
   5cb2ca9f, files without `_layer` suffix) whose edge count is <=5n-6;
   test apex(H). The corpus' chromatic numbers are SOURCE_STATED; we
   independently compute chi(H) only if it matters (any SAT apex verdict).
4. Patil data/graphs/apex_C5_34343.edges as a positive/negative cross-check
   only if resources allow (their certified verdict: non-biplanar).

## Tools, resources, stopping

Python 3.13.16, python-sat 1.9.dev15 (only for random cross-validation),
NetworkX 3.5, CaDiCaL 3.0.1 (git c6073042), drat-trim (git 2e3b2dc0).
4 cores, <=12 GB. Per-instance solver cap 3600 s; UNSAT proofs are checked
by drat-trim with a 20000 s cap. Total campaign compute cap ~8 hours.
A timeout is recorded as UNKNOWN, never as a verdict. SAT models are
decoded to E1/E2 and independently checked by NetworkX LR planarity and by
tools/check_rotation.py (from LR rotations), plus exact partition check.
Every CNF, proof verdict and hash is archived in validation/S003_MANIFEST.json
(large proofs are not committed; their SHA-256, size and checker output are).

## Meaning of outcomes

UNSAT+VERIFIED: the named fixed graph is not biplanar, conditional only on
Schnyder's theorem, the generator code (archived, hashed, reviewed) and
drat-trim. SAT: explicit layers, independently checked. Nothing beyond the
listed graphs is claimed.

## Amendment A (before any v2 run; written before the corresponding solver launch, clock time not separately logged)

The first three v1 runs (K9, apex of corpus graphs 19_12v_57e_w2 and
49_12v_53e_subdivface) were still running after 20 minutes. Encoding v2 adds
two implied constraint families, both theorems, as options of the same
generator: --euler (each layer has <=3n-6 edges; Euler) and --symtop (the
three orders of a layer are sorted by the index of their top elements; any
permutation of suitable orders is suitable). Validation: 150 seeded random
graphs (seed 5) single-layer --symtop agree with NetworkX. v1 and v2 verdicts
are reported separately. Soundness of v2 UNSAT therefore depends on Schnyder,
Euler's planar edge bound, the generator and drat-trim.

## Amendment E (written before any v4 run)

Encoding v4 (tools/biplanar_cegar.py): variables x_e (edge e in layer 1).
Clauses are of two kinds only. (i) Lemma clauses: for an edge set K that is a
subdivision of K5 or K3,3, both clauses "not all of K in layer 1" and "not all
of K in layer 2". Every lemma is re-verified by an independent structural
checker (suppress degree-2 vertices; result must be exactly K5 or K3,3) and
by NetworkX non-planarity. (ii) Lex-leader symmetry breaking x <=_lex s(x)
for a fixed list of group elements s: the layer swap, and transpositions of
consecutive closed twins (both are automorphisms of the decision problem);
the lex-least element of each orbit satisfies all of them, so this is sound.
Lemmas are found lazily (counterexample-guided: networkx Kuratowski
subgraph of a non-planar layer of the current model, plus images under the
listed symmetries). SAT = both layers planar, then checked by
tools/check_layers.py. UNSAT = final CNF re-solved by the CaDiCaL binary with
DRAT, checked by drat-trim; trust base: lemma checker, symmetry argument,
generator, drat-trim. Targets: all CAM-0003 targets as resources allow,
starting with K9 (calibration) and the order-7 survivors. Cap 3600 s each.
