## 2. Lower-route L: actual proof path and attempted new lemma

Headline needs ONE graph (labelled E=E1 disjoint E2) with each layer independently planar, proper 10-colouring and independently proof-checked no-9 instance. Row148 original n25 m131, edge SHA256 4f8dab6cb2f936ba0c69613decab88f578e4692926baa09b4d387289cefae598; 75,653 saved sound necessary Kuratowski cuts; best partial 62+63 edges and SIX unplaced. A covering by two 69-edge triangulations has overlap plus added non-source edges equal to 7: that equation concerns COVER DEFECT, not switch distance. A complete global triangulation-cover encoding, sound symmetry handling and checked native UNSAT/positive partition remain missing.

**New mathematical attempt L1 (conditional theorem, hand proof, not asserted novel).** If a biplanar graph G contains a clique K6 joined to *every* vertex of an external induced graph H, then chi(G)<=9. Proof: for h=|H|, m(G)=15+6h+m(H), n=6+h. Euler gives m(H)<=9. A triangle in H would produce K9 in G, forbidden for biplanar graphs; hence H is triangle-free. Any triangle-free graph H with <=9 edges is 3-colourable: otherwise take a vertex-minimal 4-chromatic subgraph F, which is triangle-free with minimum degree >=3. If |F|>=7, then m(F)>=ceil(3|F|/2)>=11, impossible. For |F|<=5, triangle-freeness plus Mantel's bound m(F)<=floor(|F|^2/4) contradicts minimum degree >=3. For |F|=6, m(F) must equal 9 and F is cubic and triangle-free. Pick a vertex v with three independent neighbours a,b,c; the two other vertices must each meet all of a,b,c to give their remaining two required degrees, so F=K3,3, bipartite. Contradiction. Thus chi(H)<=3 and chi(G)=6+chi(H)<=9. This conditional no-go extends the old emphasis on clique-saturated cones, does NOT exclude arbitrary alpha>=3 layered unions and does NOT prove any global upper bound. Independent adversarial proof audit: assumption the six vertices are all joined to H is ESSENTIAL; mere K6 containment is insufficient, because H=G-minus-clique can have missing join edges. The K6 vee C5 control achieves equality 9 and m(H)=5.

For direct L, S011–S014 rule out ordinary Mycielski lifts from chi>=9, one-edge chromatic-restoring swaps in bag sizes>=2, and short/saturated Sulanke core extensions. Do not claim they rule out general layer-compatible graph substitutions. Best two strategies: (L-A) **layer-first**, generate two independently verifiable near-triangulations, then maximise independently checked chromatic lower bounds; (L-B) **global row148 exact cover**, construct both triangulations with overlap+extras=7 using genuinely complete SAT/embedding proof; the saved cuts are sound but incompleteness grows with structural search. The original219 are BIASED by clique-blowup source design, so 219 UNKNOWN are not 219 independent promising random witnesses. Lower-side no-go lemma alone is not an advance.

## 3. Upper-route U: actual proof path and attempted new lemma

A counterexample to 11-colourability has a 12-critical biplanar subgraph G. Write n=|G|, m=6n-12-D, D>=0. Classical criticality gives minimum degree >=11. From sum degrees, sum_v(12-deg(v))=24+2D. If L is set of degree-11 vertices and Q=sum_{deg>=13}(deg-12), then |L|=24+2D+Q. It is invalid to conclude any L vertex reducible: S002's 42-vertex two-triangulation graph has minimum degree11 (24 degree11, 18 degree12) yet is 7-colourable. S003–S010 studies double-wheel vertices, Gallai forests, heavy K8 and long odd-block exclusions, list-palette obstructions, failed one-Kempe-swap and discharging arguments. The meaningful missing step is global **simultaneous** colouring of all low components for arbitrary high-side structures, plus treatment of D unbounded. Local Euler density/89+11D only shifts the minimum n; it supplies NO maximum n and thus NO finite all-graph reduction.

**New mathematical attempt U1 (necessary critical-local lemma, hand proof, non-novel until priority checked).** In EVERY biplanar graph with vertex v of degree exactly 11, its 11 neighbours contain at least SIX unordered nonadjacent pairs. Proof: G[N[v]] has 12 vertices, contains 11 incident edges from v and at most 6*12-12=60 edges in total, hence the neighbour subgraph has at most49 edges among binom(11,2)=55 pairs, leaving >=6 nonedges. Moreover, if G is 12-critical, EVERY proper 11-colouring of G-v uses 11 distinct colours on N(v) (or one missing colour would extend); for each nonedge xy in N(v) bearing colours a,b, there must be an a/b-Kempe path from x to y in G-v, or swapping the component of x would free a colour at v and extend. Combined with |L|>=24+2D this forces at least six distinct pairwise Kempe locks at each of >=24+2D low vertices. This is a bona fide necessary restriction for any 12-critical counterexample, but NOT a reducibility theorem. It remains necessary when two-colour paths switch planar layers. It does not prove <=11.

**Adversarial falsification of the enticing stronger claim:** 'six nonedges in N(v) imply one Kempe pair is flexible' is false without criticality: S004 gives an explicit biplanar 122-vertex graph with degree11 v whose every one of 55 neighbour colour-pairs is joined by an alternating path in a particular 11-colouring of G-v (though the whole graph is 4-colourable). This explains why a local count plus Jordan-curve intuition cannot finish U. The example does not refute a hypothetical *12-critical-only* flexibility theorem. A genuine U headline lemma would be: every 12-critical biplanar graph has an 11-colouring extension at some low vertex (contradicting criticality), proved for all D>=0; equivalently show no 12-critical biplanar graphs exist. Existing S010 simultaneous-colouring criterion is sufficient given a special independent repeated-colour set; its existence is unproved. An alternative density conjecture strong enough to force m>6n-12 for all K9-free 12-critical graphs would work, but not established at k12.

**Verdict:** Choose **L** as primary, but NOT on the simplistic assumption that a finite certificate is automatically easy. The decisive advantage is that one can in principle *finish* a positive instance with fixed checked objects, while U has unsolved infinite-case structure and no credible direct completion path at present. The most productive correction to prior D042 practice is to balance the original219 exact solver with **layer-first guaranteed-planar construction**; pursuing only row148, or just radius4/5/6, risks years of inconclusive local caps.
