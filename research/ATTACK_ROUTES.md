# Hypothesis register and adversarial branches

H-A: A 10-chromatic biplanar graph exists. Status OPEN. Construct and certify; if an exhaustive limited search fails, restrict conclusion to the preregistered domain.

H-B: Every biplanar graph is 9-colourable. Status OPEN. Try minimal 10-critical counterexample, min degree ≥9, Euler slack, embedding constraints and reducibility. A direct attempt using min degree ≤11 cannot conclude χ≤9.

H-C: A new layer-compatible construction amplifies known 9-critical graphs to 10-critical without increasing thickness. Status UNPROVED; closure of thickness≤2 under proposed operations requires separate proof.

H-D: A universal stronger degeneracy or list-colouring lemma reduces 12. Status UNPROVED; must test against the 9-chromatic known witness and arbitrary layer intersections.

H-E: A finite reduction to unavoidable critical cores can convert an exhaustive bounded computation into a universal proof. Status UNPROVED; the reduction proof is the major obligation.

Every candidate route must have precise statement, at least one failure mode, dependency graph and attempts to disprove the critical lemma.
