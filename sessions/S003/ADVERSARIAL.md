# S003 fresh internal adversarial pass

Same-agent internal review with independently written checkers. This is
not an independent human or external review.

## Protocol deviations found and how they were handled

1. Estimated clock times were first written into CAM-0003 amendment
   headings ("registered 18:52 UTC" etc.) without reading the clock. They
   were replaced by the honest wording "written before the corresponding
   solver launch, clock time not separately logged"; file mtimes in the
   scratch area are the only timing evidence.
2. The first sub-blow-up scan of the [2,6,6,6,2,2,2] survivor (300 s per
   sub-graph) and the first C5[2,6,2,6,2] run were started under the
   general wording of Amendments B/E before the explicit Amendment F was
   written. Their outputs are reported, but the deviation is recorded here.
3. The first scan's shell loop misparsed 6-bag lines (extra field); its
   6-bag results were discarded and the scan was rewritten in Python.
4. Two `pkill -f` commands matched the invoking shell and killed it (no
   data loss: one census run had to be restarted; outputs were regenerated).
5. A sentence in research/S003_UPPER_BOUND.md first claimed that at every
   degree-11 vertex of the stored 42-vertex witness the layer-1 link is
   crossed by layer-2 chords. The computation (tools/witness_double_wheel.py)
   shows this holds for 17 of 24; the text was corrected before commit.
6. Relabelled census bases first stored weights against the ORIGINAL
   graph6 string; caught before any k=8 output was used, fixed by storing
   the relabelled graph6, and checked by re-running k=7 (identical counts,
   survivor base isomorphic to FCptO).

## Mathematical challenges

- Census soundness: each filter is a theorem valid for disconnected layers
  (n>=3 caps); pruned subtrees are counted with multiplicity and the total
  is asserted equal to 8^k per base; the core reduction R5 was re-proved
  (closed weighted degree is invariant under twin merging and component
  restriction). Independent checker (numpy enumeration, MIS-cover colouring,
  triangle-branching maximisation) agrees exactly for k<=7. k=8 rests on
  one implementation — recorded as a limitation, not hidden.
- 10-chromaticity: SAT colouring "UNSAT" was never used without drat-trim;
  the FCptO hand proof was checked against all 13 vectors (N+w_h>=28).
- Survivors are NOT claimed non-biplanar or biplanar. Max-cut, max
  triangle-free (MaxSAT), codegree lemma and K9-minus-star tests all failed
  to exclude them; three 18-vertex cores and K10-C4 turned out biplanar.
- Oracle v4 soundness: lemmas re-verified by a second structural checker
  and NetworkX; CNF regenerated byte-identically from the stored lemma list;
  lex-leader: every listed permutation asserted to be an automorphism and
  only lex-least orbit representatives are kept (combining several
  constraints of one total order is sound). The v3 --twinsort option is
  deliberately exclusive with the first-edge fix and --symtop.
- Lemma 2 corner cases (triangle components, edges as components,
  isolated vertices, N=3) are handled explicitly; Lemma 4 was corrected from
  d_i to 2d_i after noticing cut-vertex angles repeat on one face.
- Szeider's alpha<=2 closure is used only as an external consistency check
  of the 570 N=19 survivors, never inside a programme theorem.
