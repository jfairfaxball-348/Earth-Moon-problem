- SRC-0042 (new) rStation paper earth_moon_36, "Counting-class fractional records,
  a vertex-transitive census, and a discharging reduction for Kral's independence
  question", forum authors, 2026-10-12, https://rstation.ai/problems/earth_moon/papers/36.
  STATEMENT_INSPECTED (abstract, theorem statements, Section 3-4 excerpts). Directly
  relevant: clique blow-ups of small bases at orders 25-29 with alpha=3 and
  chi_f up to 29/3 were searched; every collected counting-feasible candidate had a

  this programme's Lemma C / two-branch lemma). States "blob unions suffice" for
  hereditary density when all bags have size <= 8. Heuristic coverage only, by
  its own limitations section. Our S026/S027 28-vertex alpha3 source is therefore
  NOT a new idea; it overlaps this forum work.
