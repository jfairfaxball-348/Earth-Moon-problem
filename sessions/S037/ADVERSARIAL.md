# S037 adversarial checks and limits

- The symmetry theorem was checked against frozen bytes and actual registration,
  not inferred from the ID pattern. Full S11 satisfiability remains UNKNOWN.
- The original historical proof receipts were compared by exact set equality;
  no fresh historical DRAT replay was performed or claimed.
- Bitset scores were compared with direct enumeration; 613 reversible legal
  flips were tested against independent planarity. Known J1 is an alpha<=3
  positive control. Overlap, duplicates, false scores and K5 corruptions fail.
- Exact permutation clauses were tested against all 120 permutations twice,
  including four-set separation, and impossible K4 packing. Research caps
  yielded UNKNOWN, not a certificate of UNSAT or of template impossibility.
- The original kick arm can remain trapped making swaps after stagnation; one
  arm never packed. This failure is preserved. Guided all-flip refinement is a
  separate later experiment, not a retroactive reinterpretation of that arm.
- Allowing one overlap in a latent completion, then deleting it, is necessary
  to represent near-layer cases not obtainable by deleting a disjoint pair.
  Deletion scores were recomputed on the ACTUAL 77-edge layer.
- A quadrilateral's two diagonals apply to a fixed embedding. The stronger
  nonextension claim uses all 223 missing pairs and explicit subdivisions.
  It is only a claim about the fixed split, not alternative decompositions.
- Saved pairs all have checked <=9 colourings. Many independent four-sets do
  not themselves prove a graph is 9-colourable; the explicit colourings do.
- 77-edge hitting is SAT but nonplanar. A capped 78 solve is preserved UNKNOWN;
  a direct supergraph separately proves only relaxed 78-edge feasibility.
- Prior constructive flip-pair work is acknowledged; no novelty, asymptotic
  search efficiency, global coverage or probability-of-success claim is made.
- Windows S036 lifecycle/archives were not rerun or modified. Its committed
  preservation receipts remain historical; no fresh 2.64 GiB archive audit.
- Experiments stopped at registered finite/plateau/conflict limits, using much
  less than two CPU-hours. The remaining allowance does not justify a grind.
