# S042 primary-source refresh — 24 October 2026

No novelty or priority claim.

- Boutin, Gethner, Sulanke, Thickness-two graphs part one (SRC-0002): the
  author-hosted preprint text (https://academics.hamilton.edu/mathematics/
  dboutin/ThicknessTwo.pdf, read as extracted text) states 40 new 9-critical
  thickness-two graphs on 12 through 16 vertices, selected from hundreds
  generated with nauty, and an infinite family from permuted layers plus
  Hajos's construction, with a characterisation of when Hajos preserves
  thickness two. By Gallai every 9-critical graph on <=16 vertices is a join, so
  these graphs fall inside the S042 type table; those above the leaf budget
  give Euler-infeasible leaf graphs. Explicit edge lists were not extracted.
  Grade unchanged (PROOF_INSPECTED earlier for other sections).
- Gethner-Sulanke part two (SRC-0003): the 17/25/33-vertex 9-critical graphs
  were not obtained (no accessible primary text); grade unchanged. They belong
  to S042's UNRESOLVED 9-critical irreducible windows if within budget.
- Kostochka-Yancey, A Brooks-type result for sparse critical graphs
  (arXiv:1408.0846, v3 4 April 2017; SRC-0020): the ar5iv full text was read
  at statement level. Theorem 6: for k>=4 a k-critical graph meets the bound
  ((k+1)(k-2)n-k(k-3))/(2(k-1)) iff it is k-Ore; otherwise
  |E| >= ((k+1)(k-2)n - y_k)/(2(k-1)) with y_k = max{2k-6, k^2-5k+2}. k-Ore
  graphs are built from copies of K_k by DHGO compositions (delete xy in G1,
  split z of G2 into two positive-degree vertices, identify with x and y).
  Access upgraded ABSTRACT_INSPECTED -> STATEMENT_INSPECTED; proofs not read.
